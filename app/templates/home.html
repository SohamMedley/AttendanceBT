What this system is

Blockchain

Attendance is captured by scanning a rotating QR code shown by the faculty member. Every mark becomes a transaction signed with the student's secp256k1 key. Transactions are sealed into Proof-of-Work blocks linked by SHA-256 hashes, and each block carries a Merkle root committing to every record inside it.

Because the blockchain lives on one campus server, records are periodically anchored to a public ledger — only a 32-byte hash leaves the building, so attendance stays confidential while becoming provably unaltered.
