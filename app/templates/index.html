How attendance is recorded

  1. The teacher opens a roll call for a subject. A QR code appears on the projector.
  2. The QR code carries a signed token that changes every {{ settings.qr_ttl }} seconds, so a photo of it stops working almost immediately.
  3. A student scans it and enters their roll number. The mark is stored straight away.
  4. When the teacher closes the roll call, every mark is sealed into a new block, and the block is chained to the one before it by its hash.

Change one mark afterwards and the block's hash no longer matches, which breaks the chain from that block onwards. That is the whole point of putting the register on a blockchain.
